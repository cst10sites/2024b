Advantages and Disadvantages

Advantages:

  • Can be easily converted into various forms of energy like heat, light, and mechanical energy.
  • Operates with high efficiency in energy conversion, reducing waste.
  • Allows for instantaneous control and adjustment of power output, crucial for many applications.

Disadvantages:

  • Energy loss during transmission over long distances due to resistance in power lines.
  • Improper handling can lead to severe injuries or fatalities from electric shock.
  • Depending on the energy source, can contribute to pollution, habitat destruction, and greenhouse gas emissions.

Preventive Measures and Practical Applications

Current electricity is crucial for diverse applications but poses risks like transmission losses, safety hazards, and environmental impacts. Preventive measures include safety protocols and maintenance. It powers generation, transportation, communication, industry, and homes, supporting modern life and technology.
