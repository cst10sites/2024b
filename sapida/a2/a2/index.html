baklava

Baklava is a delicious dessert made of layers of fild pastry, nuts, and sweet syrup. It's popular in many Middle Eastern and Mediterranean countries. It's believed to have been invented in the Ottoman Empire around the 15th century.

recipe

  • 1 package of pastery sheets
  • 2 ½ cups of { walnut, pistachios, or almond}
  • 1 cup of melted butter
  • 1 ¼ cup of sugar
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 ¼ tablespoon of lemon juice
  • 1 table spoon of cinnamon

steps

preheat ur oven to 350°f

in a bowl, mix together chopped nuts and ground cinnamon

layer the filo sheets in a baking dish, brushing them with butter and spread the nut mixture all over it

repeat the process with the remaining ingrediants

Using a sharp knife, cut the baklava into diamond or square shapes

Bake in the preheated oven for about 45 minutes, or until golden brown

while baklava is cooking make your syrup by boiling water, lemon, and suger in a sauce pan. bring to a boil and let it simmer for 10 minutes

once the baklava is out the oven pour ur surup mixture all over it let it cool before surving
