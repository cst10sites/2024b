A brief overview - Start Here

So, what is Doctor Who? Doctor Who is a British science fiction TV show, first aired in 1963. The main plot of the show follows an immortal alien named "The Doctor" who travels through both time and space in a spaceship called the TARDIS, embarking on adventures and saving people from evil- whether that evil manifests as an extraterrestrial monster, a greedy corporation, a deadly alien virus, or forces even stranger. The Doctor's travels take them everywhere from distant galaxies millions of years in the future, to real-life historical events on earth. Always accompanying the Doctor is their faithful companion, a human who gets swept up into this thrilling life of danger and excitement.
