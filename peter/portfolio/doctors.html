Who is The Doctor?

Born and raised on the planet of Gallifrey 250 million light years away from earth, The Doctor is part of a near-immortal species of aliens called the Time Lords. They are one of the only remaining Time Lords, after the planet Gallifrey was destroyed in a war against the evil Daleks, and now travel through time and space in the TARDIS, fighting against injustice. The Doctor, like all Time Lords, is able to live for thousands of years through a biological process called regeneration. When a Time Lord is injured, old, or otherwise at a point where they would die, the process of regeneration is triggered, transforming them into a new physical form. This new, regenerated version is still the same person and retains all their previous memories, but often looks different and may exhibit slight changes in personality. Over the years, The Doctor has been played by fourteen different actors (to date); and each regeneration of The Doctor, played by a different actor, is referred to by number (such as "The Ninth Doctor" or "The Thirteenth Doctor", or simply "Nine" or "Thirteen"). The beauty of this character is that it allows for many actors to put their own spin on it and play their own unique version of The Doctor, while still retaining the whimsy and hope of the character that has endeared them to so many fans over decades.
