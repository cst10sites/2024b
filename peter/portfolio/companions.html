The Companions

The role of the companion is a crucial one throughout the story of Doctor Who. Usually a human who ends up accidentally entangled in The Doctor's adventures, the companions serve as an extension of the audience, giving The Doctor a reason to explain the events and environment out loud. Each companion has their own unique personality and relationship with The Doctor that endear them to the fanbase. Unfortunately, the companion's story is one prone to ending in tragedy. For both the sake of abbreviation and because it is where my own knowledge begins, I have included here only the companions who appear in the newer era of Doctor Who (2005 - Present). To learn more about companions from older seasons of the show, go here.(This list does not include short-term companions, companions from specials, etc.)
