Brownie Recipe

Intro

Baking is one of my favorite hobbies. It's great for calming stress, and it always feels good to take pride in something I made with my own two hands, especially if I'm sharing it with others. This brownie recipe is probably the best recipe I've ever used, and doesn't require a great deal of skill or any fancy tools. My recommendation is to serve these warm with some vanilla ice cream, to balance out the intensity of the chocolate.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Unsalted Butter
  • 1 cup Chocolate Chips, plus extra for mixing in
  • ½ cup White Sugar
  • ½ cup Brown Sugar
  • 3 Eggs
  • ½ teaspoon Vanilla Extract
  • ½ cup All Purpose Flour
  • ½ cup Cocoa Powder
  • ½ teaspoon Baking Soda
  • ½ teaspoon Cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter and chocolate in a pan. Remove from heat.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the sugar, eggs, and vanilla until thoroughly combined.
  3. Add the chocolate and butter mixture and mix until combined.
  4. Add in the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda and cornstarch, and mix until thoroughly combined.
  5. If you want, add in around ½ cup (or however much you want) of chocolate chips and mix in.
  6. Pour evenly into a medium-large square pan lined with parchment paper and top with chocolate chips.
  7. Bake at 175°C for 35-45 minutes.
  8. Let cool slightly before cutting into squares and serving.
