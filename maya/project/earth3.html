What will Earth be like in 2050 if we do Nothing to Stop Climate Change?

Climate change is something we must address in order to see a successful future for our planet. If we fail to fix already occurring climate events, climate change will only get worse. The impacts of climate change would effect everybody on Earth leading to lower quality lives for everyone. Here are some possible consequences if we fail to fix our mistakes:

SAVE

Rising temperatures and extreme weather:

  • More frequent and severe heat waves will occur

  • Average global temperatures could increase by 2.7°C or 4.9°F

  • More intense hurricanes and typhoons

  • Pro longed drought in regions such as the mediterranean, Southwest United States, and some parts of America could face extended sever droughts which could impact water supply and agricultue

  • Increased percipitation an storms will caue more frequent and severe flooding in coastal and riverine communities

Health risks

  • Warmer temperatures will expand the range of vector-borne diseases like malaria, dengue, and Zika virus

  • Heat-related illnesses from heatwaves will increase the incidence of heatstroke and cardiovascular diseases, particularly among vulnerable populations

  • Air quality will worsen due to higher temperatures and wildfires leading to respiratory issues such as asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
OUR

Melting ice and rising sea levels:

  • Greenland and Antartic ice sheets will pay the biggest contribution to sea levels rising by 2050

  • Sea levels could rise up to 1m (3.3ft) which would flood coastal cities such as New York, Mumbai, and Jakarta which would force millions of people to relocate

  • Habitats that we need to improve water quality and prevent floods like mangroves and wetlands will be lost

Economic Impacts

  • Infrastructure damage will lead to higher costs for repairs and maintenance

  • Heat stress and health issues will reduce worker productivity in sectors like agriculture, construction, and manufacturing

  • The increase of natural disasters will raise insurance rates and lead to greater financial instability
PLANET

Food and water scarcity:

  • Changes in the temperature, percipitation, and extreme weather will reduce crop yields and livestock productivity; staple crops like rice, wheat, and corn will also be affected

  • Glacial melt and altered percipiation patterns will reduce fresh water in regions that rely on glacial runoff; droughts will become more common in already arid regions which will intensify water scarcity

Social and Political Unrest

  • As sea levels rise, weather becomes more extreme, and our resource supply weakens, it will lead to large-scale migrations and potential conflicts over land and resources

  • Resource conflicts because of lack of food/water supplies could lead to conflicts within and between nations making pre-existing conflicts worse

  • The impacts of climate change will disproportionately affect low-income communities, increasing social inequalities leading to increased social unrest
