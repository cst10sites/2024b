How the iPhone was introduced

An electronic device so influential to this day, to the point where people can't imagine their lives without it, iPhone. A mobile phone used to call people, send emails, listen to music, and entertainment, iPhone. A device so compact, small, and minimal that it could go anywhere, iPhone. A device with simple, but user-friendly to all, iPhone.

Being introduced by Apple on January 9, 2007, and unveiled by Steve Jobs. It's innovative multi-touch interface, sleek design, and integrating various functions from previous devices, set a new standard on how we interact with phones. Since the launch of the iPhone, it has since changed how smartphones are produced.

How iPhone has evolved

Over the years, to today, Apple has made remarkable features, and continues to evolve and revolutionize their devices to this day. Features such as, facial recognition (Face ID), Removing the Touch ID, and moving towards a buttonless device.
