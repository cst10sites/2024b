This course is also a grade 10 course, and I have a couple of friends in grade 10 doing it too, but that is not the main point. I plan on continuing programming, mainly because technology is our future and it is constantly developing to this day, so being involved in it can lead to job opportunities in the future. I have already learned so many things in this course, but the main thing I think I have learned is the order of operations. It sounds simple, and it is, but it's extremely crucial. As an example, think about making a sandwich. You get your bread out, put the meat/condiments, etc. in the bread, and bang, you have a sandwich. But that is the thing, if we had rearranged that order and said put the meat/condiments on first, then the bread, you for sure do not end up with the ordinary sandwich. Just like with programming, if you skip/move a step, it could potentially mess up your whole code.
