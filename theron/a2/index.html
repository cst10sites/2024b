Brownie Recipe

Why i chose brownies

I chose brownies for this assignment due to the fact that my mom has always made brownies each year, so I have an attachment to them. They're one of my favorite things to bake.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup flour
  • ⅔ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ½ cup dark chocolate chips
  • ¾ teaspoons salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup canola oil or extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp water
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix wet and dry ingredients in two seperate bowls
  2. Mix wet and dry ingredients together
  3. Pour batter onto pan with parchment paper
  4. Preheat to 325°
  5. Bake for 40-45 minutes
  6. Enjoy!
