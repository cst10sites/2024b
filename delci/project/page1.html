J.K.Rowling



J.K. Rowling is a British children's author of the much-loved series of seven Harry Potter novels, originally published between 1997 and 2007. She was born on July 31, 1965 in Yate, Gloucestershire and worked as a researcher before she became a writer. Following 7 years, she faced many challenges like losing her mother, getting divorced, facing poverty. While on a train to London in 1990, Rowling came up with the concept for Harry Potter. The idea, she says, "simply fell into my head." By 2008, she became the world's highest-paid author. Currently, she is living in Edinburgh writing new novels for children.
