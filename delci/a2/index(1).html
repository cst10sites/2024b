How To Make a Blueberry Smoothie

introduction

A smoothie is a thick, smooth drink made by pureeing ingredients in a blender. It is usually made with fresh fruits, vegetables, milk and ice cubes (optional). Because it is made with cold & room temperature ingredients, it is perfect to enjoy in hot weather. For me, I like to drink smoothies to cool my body when it is really hot. The best thing about smoothies is that they are very easy to make! There are many flavors, however, the blueberry is the best smoothie flavor!

Ingredients

  • ½ cup blueberries (frozen or fresh)
  • 2 ounce plain yogurt
  • ⅜ cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • ½ of a banana
  • ⅛ cup of walnuts(optional)

Instructions

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Blend blueberries, yogurt, milk, sugar, vanilla, and walnuts in a blender until frothy.(If you want to feel the texture of walnuts, put it last.
  3. Pour into a glass and enjoy!
