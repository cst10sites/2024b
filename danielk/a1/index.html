About Me

I am Daniel, I'm 14 years old and I'm in Grade 9. I am taking this Computer Studies 10 course as an elective this semester. In my free time I enjoy working with computers by myself, doing Computer Aided Design (CAD) for 3D printing and designing, building and flying model airplanes and rockets.

I am taking this course because of my general interest in computers and programming languages and I was looking forward to programming some of my own video games. At the moment, I have learned how to use the 2009 GameMaker software for pretty basic games but I have also heavily modified the GameMaker 3 Assignment with some of my own ideas and improvements, which included better movement mechanics, an interactive main menu, multiple new different "Power-Ups" that can be used in game and even an experimental 2-Player mode.
