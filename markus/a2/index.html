How to make hot chocolate    

Introduction

           

Today we are going to be making hot chocolate. It's super simple, and it tastes really good. I highly recommend drinking it afte ra long day of skiing, or when its cold out. Overall, it is quite a delicious drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of milk
  • 2 tablespoons of hot chocolate powder
  • Spoon
