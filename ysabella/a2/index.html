Easy Sugar Cookie Recipe

I have always loved sugar cookies. They are simple yet still satisfying usually having a refined vanilla taste. I find they also balance too sweet and just sweet enough quite well too.

Amount of Time (In mins)
Prep Time 20
Cook Time 10
Total Time 30

Ingredients

  • 2 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 ½ cups white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Beat sugar, butter, egg, and vanilla in one bowl; mix flour and leaveners in another.
  2. Blend the flour mixture into the butter mixture.
  3. Roll the dough into balls and bake until the edges are golden.
